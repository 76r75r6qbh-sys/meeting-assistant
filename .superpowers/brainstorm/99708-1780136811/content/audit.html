HIG gaps & friction I found — pick what to prioritize
1

Respect the system accent color

App hardcodes an indigo (#5B6EF5) everywhere. HIG says honor the user's chosen accent. Biggest single compliance miss.

2

Recording workspace clarity

Most complex screen — live notes, controls, transcript, meter, prep pane all at once. Needs clearer hierarchy & an always-visible recording state.

3

Recorded-meeting detail layout

Summary / transcript / notes / action items competing in a dense two-column view. Reading hierarchy & the primary-action flow can be calmer.

4

Sidebar & navigation

Dashboard / To-Dos / Approvals + meeting list. Tighten grouping, badges, selection & keyboard focus to feel like Mail/Things.

5

Empty states, loading & feedback

Plain ProgressViews and bare empty screens. Add proper empty states, skeletons, and micro-feedback on approve/complete actions.

6

Approvals queue polish

Your approval inbox — filter dropdown + context menus + detail sheet. Make the approve/revise/decline flow feel first-class.

7

Settings & first-run onboarding

Long ungrouped forms; no first-run setup for vault path, LLM endpoint, permissions. A guided onboarding would cut setup friction.
